🧊🍲 Fridge AI & Sourashtra Kitchen Planner

🔗 Main App Link: https://fridge-inventory-one.vercel.app/
🔗 7-Day Meal Planner & Recipe Collection: https://fridge-inventory-one.vercel.app/recipes

Fridge AI helps households track food, plan meals, and prepare a shopping list. The web app works on desktop and mobile, and the Android app is available through its Google Play testing track.

## Play Store release notes draft — version 1.1

Plan a week of meals and make grocery shopping easier with Fridge AI.

• Create and save a 7-day Sourashtra meal plan, with breakfast, lunch, and dinner ideas.
• Compare planned ingredients with your inventory and see the estimated total quantity to buy.
• Share the shopping list with WhatsApp, Messages, Keep, and other apps, or copy it.
• Track household food inventory and review depleted or expiring items.
• Browse 323+ Sourashtra recipes and use the AI fridge/receipt scanner.
• Use the app on desktop, mobile web, or Android. English and Tamil interface options are available.

## Feature overview

1️⃣ 📅 **7-Day Sourashtra Meal Planner**
• Generate breakfast, lunch, and dinner combinations for seven days.
• Open meal details for ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and available recipe links.
• Regenerate or resample meal ideas, swap individual meals, and pin favorites to keep them in the plan. Replacing a pinned plan requires confirmation.
• Print the weekly menu or export it to Google Calendar.

2️⃣ 🛒 **Weekly Shopping List**
• Compares the saved meal plan with positive-quantity inventory and lists missing fresh ingredients.
• Adds up the recipe quantities for repeated ingredients and shows a total amount to buy when the recipe data provides one.
• Keeps pantry staples in a separate “check your pantry” section and shows depleted inventory alerts separately.
• Check items off in the app, share the list using the Android share chooser (including WhatsApp and Messages when installed), or copy it for another app.

3️⃣ 🍲 **323+ Sourashtra Recipes**
• Browse and search the recipe collection across vegetarian and non-vegetarian categories.
• Review fresh fridge ingredients separately from pantry and spice additions.
• See the Sourashtra Bhurko spice guide and available YouTube or web recipe links.

4️⃣ 📷 **AI Fridge and Receipt Scanner**
• Submit a photo of fridge/pantry items or a grocery receipt for AI-assisted item recognition.
• Review detected food names, quantities, categories, and estimated expiration dates before using the inventory.

5️⃣ 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Household Inventory and Profiles**
• Sign in to manage a household food inventory and create profiles with dietary preferences and allergy information.
• Track quantities and expiration dates, and review depleted or expired items.
• Inventory cleanup is triggered when inventory or shopping data is accessed; it is not a continuous background service.

6️⃣ 🌐 **Desktop, Mobile, and Language Options**
• Use the responsive web app on desktop or mobile, or install the Android app.
• Switch supported interface labels and common food names between English and Tamil. Some recipe titles and instructions remain in English.
• Account, inventory, and AI scanning features need an internet connection.

## Notes for release listing

• The Android app uses the same hosted web application as the website; web updates may appear after the Vercel deployment without installing a new Android bundle.
• The Play Console AAB for version 1.1 includes the native Android share integration. Users need to install/update to that version to open the Android app chooser from Share Shopping List.
• Shopping quantities are estimates based on ingredient quantities in the selected recipes. If a recipe does not specify an amount, the list says that the quantity is not specified.
